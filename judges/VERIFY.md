### Mist Bank worker (operator note)

The Zcash sender runs on the operator's machine, not on Render: `bash backend/scripts/mist-worker.sh start|stop|status`. It reads its keys from a local env file outside the repo and logs to a local file. After a reboot, run `start` again; requests queued meanwhile wait in the queue and are sent on the next pass.
